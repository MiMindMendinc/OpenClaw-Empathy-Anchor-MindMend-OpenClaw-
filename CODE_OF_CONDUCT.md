# Code of conduct

This project discusses mental health and crisis language for a **technical demonstration**.

- Be respectful. Do not shame people for asking for help.
- Do not share real private conversations or identifiable youth data.
- Do not present this software as therapy, a diagnosis, or an emergency service.
- Do not invent crisis phone numbers or hours.
- Security reports stay private until maintainers can fix them.

If someone may be in immediate danger, call or text **988** (US) or contact emergency services.

Maintainers may refuse or revert contributions that violate these expectations.
